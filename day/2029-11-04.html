Why: the reasons

Day officer: Establish

The month’s own branch rules the day: strong, assertive, a day for beginnings but too forceful for building or digging. In the 戌 month, the day whose branch equals the month branch is 建 Establish, and the officers then follow in order. Today’s branch 戌 is 0 step(s) after 戌, so it is 建 Establish. The month’s own branch rules the day: strong, assertive, a day for beginnings but too forceful for building or digging.

Path god: White Tiger

The twelve path gods rotate through the day branches. In the 戌 month, 青龙 Azure Dragon sits on 辰 days, and the others follow in fixed order. 戌 is position 7, which is 白虎 White Tiger. The six gods 青龙, 明堂, 金匮, 天德, 玉堂 and 司命 form the "Yellow Path" (黄道); the other six form the "Black Path" (黑道). Popular almanacs call a Yellow-Path day a "lucky day" (黄道吉日) and a Black-Path day unlucky. The Treatise disagrees (卷十): a path god has no activities of its own on a day. Together with good spirits it follows what they favour; together with bad spirits it follows what they forbid. So whether the day is Yellow or Black Path does not by itself make it good or bad; read the day's Suitable and Avoid lists. It matters when choosing an hour: a Yellow-Path hour suits everything and a Black-Path hour suits nothing (the good hours in the Double hours card).

Clash: Dragon

The day branch 戌 is directly opposite 辰 on the 12-branch circle, so people born in a Dragon year should be careful with important matters today. Almanacs print the clashed pillar as 壬辰: the opposite branch, plus the stem that today’s stem 戊 overcomes (天克地冲 "heaven overcomes, earth clashes"). The Sha (煞) direction comes from the day branch's three-harmony group: 申子辰 → South, 寅午戌 → North, 亥卯未 → West, 巳酉丑 → East. Today's is North.

God of Joy: Southeast

The God of Joy (喜神) is "seeing 丙": counting the day stem 戊's cycle (五虎遁), the stem 丙 falls on 辰, which lies in the trigram 巽, so the God of Joy is in the Southeast today, and the Dragon hour (丙辰) is its hour. The Treatise adds that both the direction and the hour are favourable, but must be weighed with the other spirits: a God-of-Joy hour that is also the day's Breaker (日破) should not be used. It also corrects the popular almanacs, which give the 丑 hour of 甲 days as a God-of-Joy hour because 丑 and 寅 share the trigram 艮. Checked against the hour tables printed in 卷三十二.

Noble (天乙貴人): by day northeast, by night southwest

The Heavenly Noble (天乙貴人) of a 戊 day is at 丑 by day (陽貴, the northeast) and at 未 by night (隂貴, the southwest). The Noble sits where the day stem's virtue combines (干德之合). The Treatise quotes two older ways of counting, Cao Zhengui's and the popular almanacs', and concludes that the one starting at 未 and going forward is the day (yang) Noble, and the one starting at 丑 and going backward the night Noble; day and night are divided at sunrise and sunset. This is the same rule as the rhyme 甲戊庚牛羊, 乙己鼠猴鄉, 丙丁豬雞位, 壬癸兔蛇藏, 六辛逢馬虎. The Treatise's own year tables print it the same way.

  • Many almanac apps (the lunar library's 陽貴神歌 "庚辛居離艮" and 陰貴神歌 "庚辛逢虎馬") give 庚 days the Noble at 午 (south) and 寅 (northeast), like 辛 days. The Treatise and the classic rhyme give 庚 丑 and 未.
  • Cao Zhengui (曹震圭) swaps the two: his day Noble is the Treatise's night Noble. The Treatise says this is the folk usage and rejects it.
  • The 玉匣记 (Daoist canon edition) gives one 貴神 direction a day. It matches the day or night Noble here on only 22 of the 50 surviving days, and changes irregularly for the same day stem; its rule is not stated, so it is not used.

Checked against the Noble rows printed in the Treatise’s year tables (卷十四–十八).

God of Wealth (財神): north (popular almanacs)

Popular almanacs place the God of Wealth (財神) by the day stem: 甲乙 northeast, 丙丁 southwest, 戊己 north, 庚辛 east, 壬癸 south. Today (戊) it is the north. This is not in the imperial Treatise. It is shown as popular practice (通书), from the verse 財神方位歌 as almanac apps print it; the two independent tables checked agree, but no printed 通书 has been checked yet.

  • Another version of the verse, printed in popular date-selection guides, puts 丙丁 days in the west (正西): "甲乙东北方，丙丁正西藏，戊己北方坐，庚辛正东方，壬癸南方是，我克为财乡". Its stated principle is that wealth lies in the direction of the element the day stem overcomes (fire overcomes metal, the west); the other four stems agree with both versions.
God of Fortune (福神): northeast (popular almanacs; versions differ)

Popular almanacs disagree about the God of Fortune (福神). Shown here is the default of the open-source calendar library that many Chinese almanac apps and sites are built on: 甲己 north, 乙庚 southwest, 丙辛 northwest, 丁壬 southeast, 戊癸 northeast. Today (戊) it is the northeast. A second verse gives the north today, and a third table the north. None of them is in the imperial Treatise, so treat this one as the least settled of the directions.

  • The other verse (甲乙东南是福神…): north today.
  • A third table (stem-branch reference, no source named): north today.
Fetus spirit (胎神): at the bedroom and bed and chicken roost, inside the room, centre (popular almanacs)

Households expecting a baby avoid moving, nailing, digging or repairing where the Fetus spirit (胎神) sits. Today (戊戌) it is at the bedroom and bed and chicken roost (房床栖), inside the room, centre. This lunar month it also occupies the gate and room (占门房). The object comes from two verses, one by day stem (甲己 door, 乙庚 pestle and millstone, 丙辛 kitchen stove, 丁壬 storeroom, 戊癸 bed) and one by day branch (子午 pestle, 丑未 toilet, 寅申 stove, 卯酉 door, 辰戌 roost, 巳亥 bed); the place follows a 60-day table. This is popular practice (通书, the tradition of 玉匣記), not the imperial Treatise, and the table is from an almanac library that says it corrected other copies without saying where.

The God of Wealth (财神), God of Fortune (福神) and Fetus spirit (胎神) are not in the imperial Treatise. They are shown as popular almanacs (通书) print them, marked 通书, with the versions that disagree.
