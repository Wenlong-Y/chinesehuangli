Lucky Days for a Date: May 2026

17 days in May 2026 are good for a date by the Chinese almanac, and 9 are best avoided.

How it is read: a date is read as the classical activity Gather relatives & friends (会亲友). The almanac has no dating entry. Meeting someone socially is gathering with relatives and friends. A day is good when the almanac marks it suitable and nothing is marked avoid. Verdicts from the imperial Treatise (1739), Beijing time.
