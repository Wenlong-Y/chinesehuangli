Auspicious Dates for Surgery or Medical Treatment: October 2027

6 days in October 2027 are good for surgery / treatment by the Chinese almanac, and 23 are best avoided.

How it is read: surgery / treatment is read as the classical activity Seek medical treatment (求医疗病) and Acupuncture / blood-letting (针刺). Seeking medical treatment; procedures that draw blood count as acupuncture and blood-letting. A day is good when the almanac marks it suitable and nothing is marked avoid. Verdicts from the imperial Treatise (1739), Beijing time.
