Auspicious Dates for Surgery or Medical Treatment: May 2026

7 days in May 2026 are good for surgery / treatment by the Chinese almanac, and 24 are best avoided.

Seeking medical treatment; procedures that draw blood count as acupuncture and blood-letting. A day is good when the almanac marks it suitable and nothing is marked avoid.
