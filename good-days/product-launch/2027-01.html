Lucky Days for a Product or Software Launch: January 2027

10 days in January 2027 are good for a product launch by the Chinese almanac, and 16 are best avoided.

How it is read: a product launch is read as the classical activity Open a business (开市) and Sign contracts & trade (立券交易). A first public release is opening a business, which the Treatise links to abundance; selling it is drawing up deeds and trading. A day is good when the almanac marks it suitable and nothing is marked avoid. Verdicts from the imperial Treatise (1739), Beijing time. Digital work: the earth taboos are not applied.
