Auspicious Dates to Start a New Job: November 2028

10 days in November 2028 are good for starting a new job by the Chinese almanac, and 16 are best avoided.

How it is read: starting a new job is read as the classical activity Take up a new post (上官赴任). Taking up a new post is the classical equivalent of starting a job. A day is good when the almanac marks it suitable and nothing is marked avoid. Verdicts from the imperial Treatise (1739), Beijing time.
