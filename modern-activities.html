The Chinese almanac for modern activities

The traditional Chinese almanac (黄历 Huangli, 通书 Tong Shu, 通胜 Tung Shing) lists which of about 60 classical activities are suitable (宜) or to be avoided (忌) each day. It has no entry for a software launch, a job interview or a first date. Huangli matches each modern plan to the classical activity that serves the same purpose, shows why, and then tells you which days favour it, which to avoid, and when the next good day is.

Check today for your activity

How a day is rated: if any mapped classical activity is marked Avoid, the day is to be avoided; otherwise, if one is marked Suitable, the day is good; otherwise it is neutral. The verdicts come from the imperial Treatise on Harmonizing Times and Distinguishing Directions (协纪辨方书, 1739). The mappings below are Huangli's own interpretations, not part of the classical text. You can also describe any other activity in your own words in the app and it is matched on your device.

Work, business and money

  • Start a new job 入职 / 新工作

    Read as: Take up a new post (上官赴任).

    Taking up a new post is the classical equivalent of starting a job.

  • Job interview (as the candidate) 求职面试（应聘方）

    Read as: Submit petitions or proposals (上表章). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Presenting yourself and your case to those who decide is submitting petitions or proposals to the authorities.

  • Interview candidates (hiring) 面试候选人（招聘方）

    Read as: Take in household members (hire, adopt) (进人口). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Choosing whom to bring in is taking new members into the household, the classical hiring activity.

  • Hire staff / adopt a pet 招聘 / 领养

    Read as: Take in household members (hire, adopt) (进人口).

    Taking people into the household (hiring, adopting).

  • Important meeting / pitch / proposal 重要会议 / 提案 / 路演

    Read as: Submit petitions or proposals (上表章). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Putting a proposal to people with authority is submitting petitions or proposals.

  • Sign a contract / close a deal 签约 / 成交

    Read as: Sign contracts & trade (立券交易).

    The classical activity “draw up deeds and trade”.

  • Open a shop / business 开业 / 开张

    Read as: Open a business (开市).

    The classical activity “open a business”.

  • Receive payment / invest 收款 / 投资

    Read as: Receive money / collect payments (纳财).

    The classical activity “receive wealth”.

  • Ship goods / send a delivery 发货 / 运送货物

    Read as: Open stores & pay out goods (开仓库出货财) + Set out on a journey (出行).

    The almanac has no “shipping” entry. Sending goods out is opening storehouses and paying out goods; moving them by road is setting out on a journey. Neither may be marked Avoid.

  • Ship goods by sea / boat 海运 / 船运

    Read as: Open stores & pay out goods (开仓库出货财) + Boating & crossing water (乘船渡水).

    Paying out goods, plus boating and crossing water, the old way goods went by water.

  • Travel / business trip 出行 / 出差

    Read as: Set out on a journey (出行).

    The classical activity “set out on a journey”.

Tech, software and launches

  • New product launch (first release) 新产品首发

    Read as: Open a business (开市) + Sign contracts & trade (立券交易). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    A first public release is opening a business, which the Treatise links to abundance; selling it is drawing up deeds and trading.

  • Major version upgrade 大版本升级

    Read as: Construction & breaking ground (修造动土). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Rebuilding an existing structure is construction (repair and build).

  • Routine update / minor release 日常更新 / 小版本

    Read as: Decorate walls (修饰垣墙). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Repairing and dressing walls is upkeep of what exists; the Treatise favours it on Balance days, for their sense of smoothing.

  • Bug fix / security patch / hotfix 修复漏洞 / 安全补丁

    Read as: Mend walls & fill holes (补垣塞穴). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Mending walls and plugging holes; the Treatise favours it on Close days, for their sense of closing.

  • Code cleanup / refactor 代码清理 / 重构

    Read as: Clean the house (扫舍宇) + Cleansing / remove misfortune (解除). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Sweeping the house and cleansing; the Treatise favours them on Remove days: remove the old, take in the new.

  • Data / infrastructure migration 数据 / 基础设施迁移

    Read as: Repair storehouses (修仓库). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Repairing the storehouses, where goods are kept.

  • Decommission / shut down a product 下线 / 停用产品

    Read as: Demolish buildings & walls (破屋坏垣). Digital work: the earth taboos for digging and building are not applied.

    Demolishing buildings and walls; the Treatise favours it on Destruction days, for their sense of breaking.

  • Rocket / satellite launch 火箭 / 卫星发射

    Read as: Set out on a journey (出行) + Open a business (开市).

    A launch is a departure on a far journey and the start of a major undertaking (opening a business).

Love, family and friends

  • Go on a date 约会

    Read as: Gather relatives & friends (会亲友).

    The almanac has no dating entry. Meeting someone socially is gathering with relatives and friends.

  • Propose marriage 求婚

    Read as: Agree a marriage match (结婚姻).

    Agreeing a marriage match between two families, the classical first step towards a wedding.

  • Engagement / register marriage 订婚 / 领证

    Read as: Betrothal gifts & exchange of names (纳采问名) + Agree a marriage match (结婚姻).

    Betrothal gifts and exchange of names, and agreeing the marriage match.

  • Wedding 婚礼

    Read as: Wedding (嫁娶).

    The classical activity “wedding”.

  • Sex / trying for a baby 同房 / 备孕

    Read as: Pray for children (求嗣) + Gather relatives & friends (会亲友).

    The almanac has no entry for sex itself. “Seek an heir” (求嗣) names the goal, a child, and covered the rites a family performed to ask for one; “gather relatives and friends” (會親友) covers being together. A day either favours, and neither forbids, is read here as a good day.

  • Prepare a nursery / get ready for the birth 布置婴儿房 / 待产准备

    Read as: Prepare a birthing room (修置产室) + Install a bed (安床).

    Preparing the birthing room, and installing the bed, the classical preparations before a birth.

  • Birthday party 生日聚会 / 做寿

    Read as: Gather relatives & friends (会亲友).

    The almanac has no birthday entry. A birthday party is feasting relatives and friends.

  • Party / gathering 聚会 / 宴请

    Read as: Gather relatives & friends (会亲友).

    The classical activity “gather relatives and friends”.

Home, health and everyday life

  • Move house 搬家 / 入宅

    Read as: Move house (移徙) + Install a bed (安床).

    Moving residence, and installing the bed in the new home.

  • Renovation / construction 装修 / 动工

    Read as: Construction & breaking ground (修造动土).

    The classical activity “construction and breaking ground”.

  • Surgery / medical procedure 手术 / 治疗

    Read as: Seek medical treatment (求医疗病) + Acupuncture / blood-letting (针刺).

    Seeking medical treatment; procedures that draw blood count as acupuncture and blood-letting.

  • Haircut 理发

    Read as: Haircut / shave the head (剃头).

    The classical activity “haircut”.

  • First day of school / start a course 开学 / 入学

    Read as: Begin studies / enrol (入学).

    The classical activity “begin studies”.

  • Gardening / planting 种植 / 园艺

    Read as: Planting (栽种).

    The classical activity “planting”.

  • Funeral / burial 丧葬

    Read as: Burial (安葬).

    The classical activity “burial”.

More

  • Install a kitchen stove (作灶) 作灶 / 安灶

    Read as: Construction & breaking ground (修造动土).

    Popular almanacs list 作灶 (building or installing the stove) as its own item; the Treatise’s 60 activities do not, so it is rated as construction and breaking ground. Popular rules also say “丙不修竈” (no stove work on 丙 days, from the folk rhyme the Treatise quotes in 卷三十五 but leaves out of its own rules) and offer to the stove god on 癸 days (“凡祀竈擇六癸日”, 卷三十五).
